Before you send that message

A few things worth knowing

Answers to what people usually ask before reaching out.

How fast do you actually reply?

Every message on this form goes straight to my personal inbox, not a shared support queue. I read and reply to it myself, almost always within a day, sometimes the same hour depending on timezone overlap.

Do you take on standalone automation projects?

Yes. Most of my n8n and AI automation work comes in as scoped freelance projects: invoice screening, lead scoring, reporting pipelines, that kind of thing — you can see what I build on the services page. Tell me roughly what the workflow needs to do and what tools it touches, and I'll follow up with scope and a rate.

Are you open to full-time or in-house roles too?

I'm currently three roles deep at Help Tech Co. Ltd. (social media, accounts and inventory), so a new in-house role would need to be a genuinely better fit. I'm always open to hearing about it: marketing, accounts, or automation-adjacent positions all qualify.

Do you work with clients outside Iraq?

Yes, remotely. I'm based in Erbil (UTC+3) and have shipped automation work for clients in different timezones without issue. Async communication and clear documentation are part of how I hand off every workflow, so overlap hours matter less than you'd think.

What should I actually put in the message?

Whatever gets me to a useful reply fastest: what you're trying to solve, roughly what tools or platforms are involved, and any timeline or budget constraints you already have in mind. Vague "let's chat" messages get a reply too, just a slower one.
