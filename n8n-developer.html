The short version

What does an n8n developer do?

An n8n developer designs, builds, and maintains automated workflows in n8n, connecting your apps, data, and AI models so repetitive work happens on its own. In practice that means wiring tools like Gmail, Google Sheets, Telegram, Calendar, and GoHighLevel into models such as OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini, then adding the validation and logging that make an automation safe to trust in production. Done properly, it removes hours of manual work every week without adding a system your team has to babysit.
