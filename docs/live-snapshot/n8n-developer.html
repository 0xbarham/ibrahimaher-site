The short version

What does an n8n developer do?

An n8n developer designs, builds, and maintains automated workflows in n8n, connecting your apps, data, and AI models so repetitive work happens on its own. In practice that means wiring tools like Gmail, Google Sheets, Telegram, Calendar, and GoHighLevel into models such as OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini, then adding the validation and logging that make an automation safe to trust in production. Done properly, it removes hours of manual work every week without adding a system your team has to babysit.

How it works

From process to production

  1. 01

    Scope the process

    We start with the actual, repetitive task you want gone. I map the steps, the tools involved, and where a human decision has to stay in the loop, then quote a fixed price.

  2. 02

    Build & test in n8n

    I build the workflow in n8n, wiring your apps and the right AI model, and test it against real examples until it behaves correctly on the messy edge cases, not just the happy path.

  3. 03

    Add the guardrails

    Schema validation, logging, and alerts so the automation fails loudly instead of silently, plus a human approval step wherever money or risk is involved.

  4. 04

    Hand over, documented

    You get the workflow, a plain-language explanation of how it runs, and documentation so your team is never stuck if I am not around.
